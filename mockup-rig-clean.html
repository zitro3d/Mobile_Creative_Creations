DESIGN DECISIONS — V1

  • Cab on top: trapezoidal armor block, lighter cream cap on top, sloped front edge, dark visor slit with a faint cyan steering-wheel ring inside (visible at 4× and up).
  • Body: cyan elongated hull with horizontal yellow caution stripe along the lower edge, top highlight band (lighter cyan), bottom shadow band (darker cyan), tapering slightly toward the front nose.
  • Tail: small fin rising from the back-top of the body, dark grey armor.
  • Engine pod: separate dark-grey module bolted under the rear half of the body, with a cyan glow vent visible at the back where the exhaust exits.
  • Exhaust direction: flame exits the BACK of the engine pod (going down and slightly backward — the artwork's directional flame), not straight down.
  • Connection plate: small riveted dark plate at the back-bottom of the body where the chain attaches.
  • Cargo + chain: kept as-is — you said these read well. Just slightly cleaned up to match the rig's smooth-edge style.

WHAT TO REVIEW

  • Cab proportions — is it the right size relative to body?
  • Engine pod placement — back enough? Too far?
  • Tail fin — visible, or needs to be larger / smaller?
  • Flame direction — backward-and-down or pure-down?
  • Color palette — close enough to the reference, or some tones off?
  • Connection plate — visible at the back-bottom?
