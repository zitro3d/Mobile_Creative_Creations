DLX HAULER · LEVEL ART · v435-pixel

SUNSET WASTES

Daily-mode level redrawn to match the ROAD KILL rig's pixel-art aesthetic. Hard-edge fillRect blocks, no gradients, no blurs, no smooth curves. Same silhouettes as the current level so hitboxes and parallax timing don't shift — just a chunky 8-bit skin over every element.

FULL SCENE · 360 × 640 (game native)
Pixel-perfect 1:1 at game scale. Shows the full sky → horizon → terrain stack with ambient props, obstacles, and rivals composited together to verify everything reads cohesively.
SKY · HORIZON · TERRAIN
Sky bands (4 horizontal strips dithered at the seams) replace the current 4-stop linear gradient. Moons stay crescent + small-full but rebuilt with pixel-aligned circles. Dunes step in 2-px increments. Citadel renders as flat sandstone blocks with hard window pixels + boxy smoke puffs. Terrain band gets a stepped profile + scattered pebble pixels.
AMBIENT PROPS
Surface-level scenery sprites. All at the same pixel scale as the rig (1 native pixel = 1 game pixel). Multiple rock variations so no two outcroppings look identical.
STATIONS · BOOKEND LANDMARKS
DLX Dispatch Center (where the rig takes off) and the Good Buddy Diner (where the rig lands at the end of the run, greeted by the 6-armed alien dispatcher). Both pixelated to match the rest of the art.
OBSTACLES + HAZARDS
Scrap tower, air wreck, magnet recall box, tunnel entrance + the underground tunnel interior (with bones + collectibles), fireball projectile, and the burrowing tentacle monster bursting from the ground. All animated where motion sells the threat.
RIVALS · GROUND OPS · DRONES · THIEVES
Three enemy archetypes. Ground hauler (rust pickup with magnet mast), security drone (twin-rotor hovercraft), and aerial thief (swept-fighter rival rig). All in the same pixel-art lexicon as the player rig — block-stepped silhouettes, flat fills, hard outlines.
COLLECTIBLES + PICKUPS
Everything the player grabs during a run — cargo crates, gems, coins, fuel, badges, powerups (turbo / shield / magnet), score popups. All animated where collection feedback matters.
ENVIRONMENTAL PARTICLES
Pixel-art versions of all environmental + feedback particles: drifting dust motes (sky ambient), rising embers (heat sources), collision spark bursts, sand spray (wheels), smoke wisps, damage stars, cargo-loss puffs. All animated so the loops can be reviewed.

CONVERSION RULES (APPLIED TO EVERY ELEMENT)

  • imageSmoothingEnabled = false on every draw.
  • Integer pixel alignment — all fillRect coords passed through | 0 or Math.round.
  • No linear/radial gradients — replaced with 2-4 flat color bands.
  • No shadowBlur — replaced with 1-px offset color blocks for "glow."
  • No Path2D curves — replaced with stepped fillRect silhouettes.
  • Palette unified with the rig's roadkill palette: peach/tan/rust on top, charcoal outlines, yellow/orange hazards.

WHAT STAYS THE SAME

  • Sky color stops (peach → rust) — same hue intent, just stepped instead of smooth.
  • Element silhouettes and dimensions — hitboxes preserved.
  • Parallax speeds and spawn cadence.
  • Daily-mode HUD and level banner.
