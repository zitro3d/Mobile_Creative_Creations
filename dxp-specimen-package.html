5 Capture Checklist

  1. Open the live build in Safari on iPhone or in Chrome DevTools mobile view (iPhone 14 / 390×844). URL: dumplord-x-press-mobile-game.html above.
  2. Specimen A — Splash: wait for the splash to settle, screenshot the full viewport. Verify the DUMPLORD X-PRESS™ wordmark, the tagline, and the PLAY NOW button are all visible. Save as spec-A-splash.png.
  3. Specimen B — Manual: tap the ? in the top-left to open the manual. Screenshot the panel showing "DUMPLORD X-PRESS MANUAL" and the rookie blurb. Save as spec-B-manual.png.
  4. Specimen C — Gameplay (optional): from splash, tap PLAY NOW, fly a few pillars, screenshot a gameplay frame. Save as spec-C-gameplay.png.
  5. Drawing: from Section 1 above, download either the color or B&W drawing (one only). USPTO TEAS only accepts one drawing file.
  6. Filing: open USPTO TEAS Plus, paste the copy blocks from Section 3 into the matching fields, upload the drawing and one of the specimens. Done.
