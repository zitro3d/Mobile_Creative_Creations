# Wondrous Grid — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: May 18, 2026**

**Everything Wondrous Inc.** ("Everything Wondrous", "we", "us", "our") operates **Wondrous Grid** (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what data we do — and do not — collect when you use the Service.

## TL;DR

Wondrous Grid runs entirely in your browser. We do not have a server that receives, processes, or stores your artwork. We do not use analytics or tracking. We do not require an account.

## 1. What we collect: nothing on our servers.

Wondrous Grid is a client-side application. Your projects, artwork, animation frames, layers, palettes, presets, settings, and imported assets are stored **only in your own browser**, using:

- **`localStorage`** — small per-project state such as canvas dimensions, selected palette, tool preferences, and project names
- **`IndexedDB`** — larger binary data such as saved frames, imported images, GIF animations, and team-shared assets

These browser storage mechanisms are visible only to you and to scripts running on the Wondrous Grid domain. We do not have access to them.

## 2. What we do NOT do

- We do not run analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or fingerprinting scripts.
- We do not set first-party or third-party cookies for tracking. (We may use a single first-party cookie in the future for purely functional reasons such as preserving your theme preference across sessions; if so, this policy will be updated and the cookie disclosed before activation.)
- We do not log, store, or transmit your IP address on our infrastructure.
- We do not sell, share, rent, or monetize personal data.
- We do not train AI or machine-learning models on your artwork or usage.
- We do not have a backend that receives your Content.

## 3. Optional third-party integrations

The Service may offer optional integrations that, **only when you explicitly enable them**, cause your browser to communicate directly with third-party services. We do not proxy, mediate, or log these requests.

Currently:

- **GitHub repository browsing.** If you enter a public GitHub repository URL into a project, your browser fetches metadata from `api.github.com` and image bytes from `raw.githubusercontent.com`. Those requests are made by your browser, not by us. GitHub's privacy policy governs that interaction.

If new optional integrations are added in the future, they will be explicitly opt-in and disclosed in this section before they ship.

## 4. Hosting and server logs

The Service is delivered as static files from a content-delivery network (currently Cloudflare Pages). The CDN provider may automatically receive standard server-log information (IP address, request time, user agent, referrer) as part of normal web traffic, in accordance with its own privacy policy. We do not aggregate, analyze, profile, or correlate this data with individual users, and we do not retain copies of these logs.

## 5. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Service, contact us and we will take appropriate action.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not collect or store your personal information, there is no personal data on our servers for you to access, correct, delete, port, or restrict. To remove any data the Service has placed in your own browser, clear your browser's site data for the Wondrous Grid domain. This will permanently delete all of your projects, artwork, and settings stored locally.

If applicable privacy law (for example, GDPR, CCPA) grants you specific rights, you may exercise them by contacting us at the address below.

## 7. Security

Because all of your Content stays in your browser, the primary risk to your work is browser-level (browser misconfiguration, malicious extensions, clearing site data). We recommend:

- Exporting and backing up important projects regularly (use **File → Save** or the export buttons)
- Using a trusted, up-to-date browser
- Avoiding installing untrusted browser extensions on the domain hosting Wondrous Grid

## 8. International users

If you access the Service from outside the United States, you understand that the Service is operated by a U.S. company. Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, no cross-border data transfer of personal data occurs through ordinary use of the Service.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

If our data-handling practices materially change — for example, if we add user accounts, cloud sync, analytics, or paid features that require collecting personal information — we will update this policy and prominently notify users before the change takes effect.
