DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Old version was working hard but in template-territory: a SaaS-style hero card with a 3-stat row, a uniform card grid for completed routes, and pulsing glow as the only motion. New version commits to the brand: orange carries the surface, decoration is diegetic (it looks like it could be in the wasteland), and motion has a job.

WHAT CHANGED, WHY

BEFORE Career strip with "rig + 3 stats + GARAGE button" — every SaaS dashboard you've ever seen.
AFTER · DRIVER ID Looks like the back of a real trucker's license. Driver number, class, license-expiry, total scrap. Asymmetric. Tells a story.
BEFORE 3 stat tiles inside the hero (BEST · HIGH SCORE · ATTEMPTS) — the SaaS hero-metric template.
AFTER · CARGO LOG Inset paper-style readout with dotted leaders, monospaced tabular digits. Reads as paperwork the dispatcher handed you, not as a dashboard.
BEFORE ★☆☆ rendered as 3 large stars with no spatial meaning.
AFTER · STAR PROGRESS A horizontal track with 3 checkpoints. Earned stars are glowing pins, missing stars are empty rings. Same data, more legible at a glance.
BEFORE "Star Hunt" 5-card grid of completed routes. Identical-card-grid anti-pattern.
AFTER · THE ROAD SO FAR A horizontal scrolling road. Each route is a milepost pin colored by zone. Completed pins show stars, NEXT pin pulses with a "you are here" arrow. Tells the player's journey, not their inventory.
BEFORE Pulsing glow on the hero card. Decoration without purpose.
AFTER · DIEGETIC MOTION Construction-tape stripe scrolls horizontally (looks like a real conveyor moving). Aurora drifts at the horizon. Snow falls. PUNCH IT button has an idle ignition glow that pulses like a heat-shimmer. Everything moves because the world moves, not to grab attention.

COLOR / TYPE / LAYOUT (the impeccable lens)

  • Color strategy: COMMITTED. DLX orange-red carries ~40% of surface (badge, hero border, ignite button, accents). The wasteland purple-black backdrop is the canvas the orange burns through. No tinted-neutral-with-one-accent restraint pretending to be brand-y.
  • OKLCH everywhere. No #000 or #fff. Every neutral is tinted toward purple (chroma 0.02–0.05). Look at the page background: it's not black, it's deep wasteland purple.
  • Type: 3-tier scale-and-weight contrast. Impact poster-headers (FROSTFALL PASS, HAULER) at 26–32px. Space Mono diegetic readouts (DRIVER #4711, 14,200, 2,140) at 9–14px tabular. Helvetica Neue body for everything else. No middle-tier soup.
  • Spacing varies on purpose. Hero card breathes (16px padding). Manifest is tight (10px). Driver ID is mid-density. Same padding everywhere = monotony — broken.
  • No nested cards. Hero is a card; the manifest inside is an inset, not another card. Star bar isn't a card. Road is a card; pins inside aren't cards. Cards that contain cards are an automatic loss.

MOTION (designed, not decorative)

  • Construction tape: 14s linear scroll left. Reads as a conveyor belt running idle.
  • Aurora: 26s ease-in-out drift, alternating direction. Slow and atmospheric.
  • Snow: 8–13s linear fall, 10 staggered particles. Each stagger means no synchrony — feels alive.
  • PUNCH IT idle: 2.4s ease-out-expo glow pulse on a radial behind the button. Like a vibrating engine. Settles to static on hover.
  • NEXT pin: 2.2s vertical bob — gentle, not bouncy. The "you are here" arrow above bobs in sync.
  • All easing: ease-out-expo / ease-out-quint. No bounce, no elastic. Per the law: only transform and opacity animated. Layout properties never.
