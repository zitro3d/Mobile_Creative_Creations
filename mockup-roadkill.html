HERO RIG · AUDIT (what's working / what to push)

  • Reads instantly — silhouette is clean: low wedge body, bubble at back, jet flame off the tail. You know what direction it's facing without thinking.
  • Bubble cockpit is the signature — the diagonal white reflection sweep + cyan steering ring + black driver pip make it the visual anchor. Keep this. Variants riff the dome shape but the "glass + reflection" recipe stays.
  • Engine pod is doing a lot of work — the 3-stage bottle (bell → waist → nose) reads as mechanical / deliberate, not just a blob. Worth preserving in variants that want a "serious hauler" feel.
  • Antenna sway is subtle but huge — gives the rig life when it's parked. Variants below all use it but with different sway rates per personality.
  • Push opportunities: only one fin (design notes call for "dual angular tail wings"), only 3 headlights (notes say 4), no visible hood mascot, no front bumper chrome, no curled exhaust pipes off the engine side. Some fleet variants restore these; others lean into the simplified silhouette.
  • Color saturation sits in the "weathered" zone — readable on dark backgrounds, less so on bright sky. HELLFIRE / NEON RAT push toward higher chroma; MOSS BOSS / GHOSTRIDER push toward muddier.

FLEET DESIGN AXES

  • Color — teal · rust · gunmetal · olive · purple · gold · bone · ice.
  • Shape — body length/height varies, fins go from single triangle → twin mohawk → swept aero spoiler → boxy slab.
  • Engine — bottle (3-stage) · fat bell · long jet tube · ring rocket · alien bulb · twin bottles · twin jets.
  • Animation — flame flicker rate, antenna sway speed, body bob, frost-particle trails, underglow pulse.
  • Style — wasteland hot-rod, demon brute, stealth racer, military workhorse, cyberpunk wedge, showboat, alien smuggler, arctic courier.
