ROAD KILL

Two parallel variations of the same rig — the cyan wasteland hot-rod from the reference IP. Variation A: chunky 16-bit pixel-art. Variation B: pushes toward the 3D reference's polished illustrated look. Both share the same design DNA — bubble cockpit at back-center, dual tail wings, orange grille with quad headlights, ROAD KILL plate, bottle-shaped engine pod with curled chrome exhaust, antenna, hood mascot, weathered teal palette.

SHARED DESIGN ELEMENTS (BOTH VARIATIONS)

  • Bubble cockpit at back-center — domed glass canopy with curved top, driver silhouette + steering wheel visible inside.
  • Dual angular tail wings at the back-top, sticking up.
  • Orange grille + 4 yellow round headlights across the front face.
  • ROAD KILL license plate — yellow rectangle, black text, mounted on the front bumper.
  • Bottle-shaped engine pod under back-center — chunky bell at rear, narrower middle, doesn't extend to front.
  • Curled chrome exhaust pipes coming out the side of the engine (separate from the main thrust nozzle).
  • Antenna rising from the back-right with a small bead at the top.
  • Hood mascot — small black sphere on the front hood.
  • Visible wheel + sparks hint underneath the back end.
  • Bigger / hotter flame with white-hot core trailing horizontal-left from the rear nozzle.
  • Weathered dusty teal palette — less saturated than my prior cyan, more lived-in.

WHAT'S DIFFERENT BETWEEN THE TWO

  • A (pixel): hard-edge fillRect blocks, no gradients, chunky discrete pixels. Matches your 16-bit pixel reference style.
  • B (3D-pushed): linear and radial gradients on body for volume, curved cockpit dome via Path2D, soft drop-shadow under rig, more color stops on flame, smoother edges. Closer to the 3D reference render.
