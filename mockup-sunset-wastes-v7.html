NEO-16-BIT BATCH

High-fidelity pixel art for the 4 "big" assets, rendered in our house Neo-16-bit style: hero artwork against pure void, strict 3-step shading, geometric square energy, zero softness.

NEO-16-BIT STYLE RULES

  1. 1 · GREEBLING High-density mechanical detailing: rivets, panel seams, vents, instrument lights, antenna nodes. Surfaces are never blank — every flat panel earns a detail.
  2. 2 · ORTHOGRAPHIC SIDE-VIEW Pure profile projection. No foreshortening, no 3/4 perspective. Every sprite reads as a flat side elevation against the void.
  3. 3 · 3-STEP VALUE SHADING Every material uses exactly three discrete values: Shadow, Mid, Highlight. Plus a black outline and any accent (lights / effects). No ramps, no mid-tones.
  4. 4 · GEOMETRIC SQUARE ENERGY Flames, exhaust, beams, sparks, antigrav: rendered as stepped square blocks, never soft round puffs. Hard rectangles of decreasing size make the falloff.
  5. 5 · ZERO SOFTNESS Every pixel is a hard-edged color block. No alpha, no AA, no gradients between colors. Transitions snap from one solid value to the next.
  6. 6 · HIGH CONTRAST VOID Hero shots sit on pure black (#000000). The sprite is the only light source. Yellow chrome bevel framing tells you "this is the canonical reference."
